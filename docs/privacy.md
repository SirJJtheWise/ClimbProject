# Privacy Policy — Crux Check

**Last updated: 18 September 2026**

## The short version

Crux Check does not collect anything. There are no accounts, no servers, no
analytics and no advertising. Everything you enter stays in the app's private
storage on your own device.

## What the app stores, and where

Crux Check asks you for information in order to estimate a climbing grade:

- Body data: sex, height, arm span, date of birth, bodyweight and, if you
  choose to enter it, body fat percentage
- Test results: the loads, times, edge sizes, rung numbers and other figures
  you record
- Your preferences and the date you accepted the safety notice

All of it is written to a database inside the app's own private storage on your
device. None of it is transmitted anywhere. The developer cannot see it and has
no way to request it.

## What the app does not do

- It does not create an account or ask for an email address
- It does not contain analytics, crash reporting or tracking of any kind
- It does not contain advertising
- It does not request any Android permissions
- It does not read your contacts, location, photos, files or health records
- It does not share or sell data, because it does not collect any

## Leaving the app

The only outbound link is the optional "Buy a coffee" button on the results
screen, which opens a third-party donation page in your browser. Crux Check
sends nothing with that link. If you follow it, that site's own privacy policy
applies to whatever happens there, and any payment is handled entirely by them.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling Crux Check deletes its database along with it. You can also clear
everything from inside the app, under the profile menu, without uninstalling.
Because nothing is sent anywhere, there is no copy left behind for anyone to
delete.

Note that this also means there is **no backup**. If you uninstall the app or
reset your device, your recorded history is gone.

## Children

Crux Check is not intended for children, and the safety notice asks anyone
under 18 not to use it without medical supervision, because maximal finger
loading can damage growth plates that have not finished closing. No data is
knowingly collected from anyone, of any age, because no data is collected at
all.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published here and the
date above will change. A change that affects what the app does with your data
will also be reflected in the app's Play Store data safety section.
